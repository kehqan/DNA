How it works
3 steps · about 20 seconds
Step 1 of 3
Name the meeting & configure
Type the meeting name — Copilot will find it. Choose your output destination and toggle the sections you need for this particular recap.
Step 2 of 3
Copy your prompt
Hit Copy Prompt. The full structured instruction is assembled invisibly from your choices. Then click Open Outlook to jump straight there.
Step 3 of 3
Paste into Copilot & get your recap
Open the Copilot sidebar in Outlook. Paste with Ctrl+V and send. Copilot finds the meeting, reads the transcript or chat, and returns a structured recap ready to drop into your chosen destination.
