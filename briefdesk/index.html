How the desk works
  • Intake turns a wall of model text into a clean, collapsible outline — nothing is lost, everything is navigable.
  • On the Desk, you promote the lines that matter into Angles, Sources, People and Keywords, add a note, and rank them by dragging.
  • Long URLs collapse into source chips with the outlet's domain; tables become sortable and filterable.
  • The finished Brief gives a reporter the verdict up top, a scroll spine down the side, and a numbered source ledger at the foot.
