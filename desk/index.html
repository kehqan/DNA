🖋️ How Desk + 🧬 BriefDesk work together

🖋️ Desk

Your writing home. A clean, journalist-friendly editor — formatting, RTL/LTR, links, export to Word. Works with or without a brief attached.

🧬 BriefDesk

The research workspace. Turns a Copilot answer into a structured, promotable brief — priorities, sources, search demand — that Desk can pull from while you write.

Copilot→/agentmode→BriefDesk→Desk
1
Copy the research workflow, once
Teaches Copilot the 4-agent newsroom research process.
Save the following workflow in your memory under the command: /agentmode…
2
Paste it into Copilot, one time
It confirms the workflow is saved as /agentmode — you won't need to do this again.
3
Research any topic
Type in Copilot: /agentmode your topic here
4
Turn it into a brief, then write
Copy Copilot's answer → BriefDesk's ＋ New brief → paste. From there, promote what matters and click Write your piece to land here in Desk with the brief pinned alongside you.
