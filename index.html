How it works
3 steps · takes about 20 seconds
Quick guide
Step 1 of 3
Configure your tool
Choose your settings — article format, language, focus modules, or whatever the tool asks for. Your answers silently shape the prompt in the background.
Step 2 of 3
Copy your prompt
Hit Copy Prompt — the tool has assembled everything in the background. You never see the raw prompt. Then click Open Outlook to jump straight there.
Step 3 of 3
Paste into Copilot
In Outlook, open the Copilot sidebar. Paste with Ctrl+V and hit send. Copilot reads your M365 context and delivers a structured, editorial-grade response.
