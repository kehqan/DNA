What we hold to

3 commitments

Nothing publishes itself.

Every tool here stops short of the send button, on purpose. A person signs off, every single time.

Everything it gives you is a draft.

Output is a starting point to argue with, never an answer to accept. The best use of these tools is disagreeing with them.

The byline stays human.

Credit and accountability sit with the journalist. If it carries a name, a person stood behind it.
